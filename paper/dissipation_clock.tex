\begin{abstract}
For the original unforced whole-space equation we derive a direct enstrophy
bound with exponential coefficient $4S^3/(3\nu^2)$ times the accumulated
cubic dissipation.  It provides a nonendpoint suffix for strict signed
pressure absorption and a single-barrier extension allowing a logarithmic
dissipation deficit.  All component proofs are included.  The arbitrary-data
pressure estimate remains unproved; independent audit of these sharpenings
is pending.
\end{abstract}
