# All-orders spectral cage for a four-growing-parent source lattice

Date: 2026-09-11. Repository input:
`itpplasma/navier@f5917aa85091424c84ec89341c2d6520e2a3c6be`.

**Status: exact source-reference spectral/convolution theorem.** The first four-parent pure-decaying synthesis had a growing cubic descendant. That failure is not universal. A second rational geometry makes the complete integer convolution lattice spectrally closed in a much stronger sense: the **only linearly growing Fourier modes anywhere in the lattice are the four original growing parents and their reality partners**. Every other nonzero lattice frequency is linearly damped by the same frozen source-reference operator.

The two designated parent pairs still synthesize a nonzero pure decaying daughter at quadratic order. Thus all higher nonlinear products may populate infinitely many modes, but none introduces a new linearly unstable frequency. This converts the next problem from finite-jet cancellation into a full stable-complement / unstable-manifold problem.

The theorem concerns the frozen source reference system. Finite-`L` time dependence, source mean/pressure, whole-space localization and one physical initial trace are not yet included.

## 1. Geometry and one common reference background

Normalize the source reference undamped eigenvalue to `lambda_0=1`, set `c_0=-1`, and take scalar viscous coefficient

    mu=3/5.                                                 (1.1)

For tilt `s`, use the source eigenpolarizations

    a_+(s)=(1,-sqrt(1+s^2),-s),
    a_-(s)=(1,+sqrt(1+s^2),-s).                           (1.2)

Choose

    c=1/20,
    d_A=9/20,
    d_B=3/20.                                              (1.3)

The four `b=1` growing-parent frequencies are therefore

    p_1=( 1/2, 0,1),
    p_2=(-2/5, 0,1),
    p_3=( 1/5, 0,1),
    p_4=(-1/10,0,1).                                      (1.4)

The designated sums agree:

    p_1+p_2=p_3+p_4=(1/10,0,2).                           (1.5)

As in the preceding synthesis theorem, write each pair output at (1.5) in the daughter eigenbasis. Strict convexity of `sqrt(1+s^2)` makes the negative/positive coordinate ratio strictly larger for `d_A` than for `d_B`. Weight the pair products by the opposite positive coordinates. The growing daughter coordinate cancels exactly, while the decaying coordinate is positive.

The checker reduces the latter to

    81[-3 sqrt(40501)-5 sqrt(2005)
       +2 sqrt(11629)+6 sqrt(10426)]/8040050 >0.          (1.6)

Thus the quadratic target is a nonzero multiple of

    a_-(1/20).                                             (1.7)

## 2. Exact convolution lattice

The four parent tilts have the representation

    s=(3m-1)/10,
    m in {-1,0,1,2}.                                      (2.1)

Therefore every parent frequency is of the form

    k_(m,1)=((3m-1)/10,0,1).                              (2.2)

Reality supplies their negatives. Since Fourier convolution adds wavevectors, every frequency generated at **any nonlinear order** belongs to

    L={ k_(m,z)=((3m-z)/10,0,z) : m,z in Z }.             (2.3)

Indeed

    k_(m_1,z_1)+k_(m_2,z_2)=k_(m_1+m_2,z_1+z_2).          (2.4)

This is exact arithmetic closure, not a finite-depth enumeration.

## 3. Frozen source-reference spectrum on the lattice

For `z!=0`, write

    k_(m,z)=z(s,0,1),
    s=(3m-z)/(10z).                                       (3.1)

The positive reference eigenbranch has real growth rate

    gamma_+(z,s)
      =1/sqrt(1+s^2)-mu z^2(1+s^2),                      (3.2)

while the negative branch has the first term negated and is always strictly more damped.

For `|z|>=2`, independently of `s`,

    gamma_+(z,s) <= 1-mu z^2
                   <=1-12/5 <0.                          (3.3)

For `z=0`, every nonzero radial lattice frequency has no positive stretching in the source reference limit and is strictly viscously damped. The zero Fourier mean is not generated by the incompressible quadratic self-interaction from zero-mean data.

It remains only to inspect `|z|=1`. By reality it suffices to take `z=1`, for which

    s=(3m-1)/10.                                          (3.4)

The scalar function

    f(q)=1/q-(3/5)q^2,
    q=sqrt(1+s^2),                                        (3.5)

is strictly decreasing in `q`, hence in `|s|`. Exact endpoint checks give

    gamma_+(1,1/2)=2/sqrt(5)-3/4 >0,                      (3.6)

    gamma_+(1,7/10)=10/sqrt(149)-447/500 <0.              (3.7)

The four lattice sites

    m=-1,0,1,2

have tilts

    -2/5, -1/10, 1/5, 1/2,                               (3.8)

precisely the four parents. The next site to the left is `-7/10`; the next to the right is `4/5`. By monotonicity, all other `z=1` sites are damped.

Therefore:

**Theorem (all-orders spectral cage).** In the complete convolution lattice (2.3), the only nonzero linearly growing modes of the frozen source-reference operator are the positive eigenbranches at the four parent frequencies (1.4) and their conjugate reality partners. Every other lattice mode/eigenbranch is strictly damped.

## 4. Why this changes the prehistory problem

The previous cubic-wall example failed because its first new cubic frequencies were themselves linearly growing. The present geometry excludes that mechanism at **every order**. Cubic, quartic and arbitrarily high nonlinear products can feed back into the four unstable parent sites or create stable modes, but they cannot open a fifth unstable Fourier channel.

This is exactly the spectral hypothesis under which a Lyapunov--Perron / local unstable-manifold construction is natural for the **full infinite reference lattice**: prescribe the four unstable amplitudes as `t -> -infinity`, retain every stable Fourier mode, and let the stable complement be generated causally by the complete quadratic convolution. The desired decaying daughter (1.7) is then expected to appear as a quadratic stable-manifold coefficient rather than as an independently initialized pulse.

That is the next exact discriminator. One must not replace it by a finite Galerkin closure. A useful theorem would establish, in a Wiener/Sobolev sequence space, a full backward-eternal small solution of the frozen reference system whose stable part is an analytic function of the four unstable amplitudes, with the coefficient at (1.5) equal to the pure negative branch above. A failure would need an actual spectral/functional obstruction, not merely the existence of infinitely many damped modes.

## 5. Scope

This theorem does not say the source's full physical prehistory is solved. The frozen reference multiplier suppresses finite-`L` coefficient variation, moving labels, exterior transport, mean/pressure feedback and localization. Nor does spectral damping bound a stable mode forced by arbitrarily large unstable amplitudes. The prospective unstable-manifold module is intrinsically local/small-amplitude and must later be matched to the source's growth interval and common physical trace.
