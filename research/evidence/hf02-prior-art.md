# Prior-art comparison for the fixed-cutoff signed (L^3) high-frequency term

This file records primary-source checks relevant to the first HIGH-PRESSURE gap in `PLAN.md`. The target there is, for a fixed dyadic cutoff (J),

\[
\int_0^\tau \int_{\mathbb R^3}p_{>J}\,u\cdot\nabla |u|\,dx,dt
 \leq \theta\nu\int_0^\tau D_3(t)\,dt+A_{\rm high}(\nu,u_0,H,J),
\qquad \theta<1,
\]

for every arbitrary-data trajectory and every (\tau<\min(H,T_*)). Here (D_3) is the weighted (L^3) dissipation from the project frequency note. A criterion that assumes the critical norm is already finite, or that gives continuation only after a smallness hypothesis, does not establish this target.

## Comparison conclusion

None of the inspected sources proves the project’s (HF) estimate for arbitrary data. Bradshaw–Grujić supplies a useful frequency-localized continuation template but assumes velocity Besov information. Zhou supplies pressure regularity criteria after assuming pressure control. Cox proposes a critical-endpoint frequency architecture whose available tail bound consumes the critical norm and whose global claim has unresolved references. The Taghizadeh item is unavailable. These are prior-art leads only; they do not close the fixed-cutoff signed (L^3) pressure estimate.

Zhou’s criterion and weighted pressure calculation were inspected in the accessible primary source record/PDF excerpt linked by the DOI; because a stable numbered full text was not available in that inspection, no theorem number is supplied.
