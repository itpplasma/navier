# Prior-art comparison for the fixed-cutoff signed \(L^3\) high-frequency term

This file records primary-source checks relevant to the first HIGH-PRESSURE gap in PLAN.md. The target is, for a fixed dyadic cutoff \(J\),

\[
\int_0^\tau\int_{\mathbb R^3}p_{>J}\,u\cdot\nabla|u|\,dx\,dt
\leq\theta\nu\int_0^\tau D_3(t)\,dt+A_{\rm high}(\nu,u_0,H,J),
\qquad \theta<1,
\]

for every arbitrary-data trajectory and every \(\tau<\min(H,T_*)\). Here \(D_3\) is the weighted \(L^3\) dissipation in the project frequency note. A criterion assuming the critical norm is already finite, or requiring smallness, does not establish this target.

## Comparison conclusion

None of the inspected sources proves the project’s fixed-cutoff signed \(L^3\) pressure estimate for arbitrary data. Bradshaw–Grujić supplies a valid velocity-frequency continuation criterion with additional Besov input. Zhou supplies repaired conditional pressure criteria with explicit endpoint qualifications. Cox D.5 is a stated local tail candidate whose budget consumes critical norms; G.5 cannot be imported because of the concrete G.30 threshold defect and unresolved references. Taghizadeh remains inaccessible. These leads do not close HIGH-PRESSURE.
