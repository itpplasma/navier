## 1. Exact finite-L realization of the four caged parents

Use the unrounded source phase law from
`2026-09-10-source-phase-closure.md`, divided by the common positive scale `B_s`.
At a common fast time `v`, a clock-shifted symmetric source pair with center `c`
and half-separation `u` has normals

    p_+(u,v)
      =(c+u(1+v/L), -u/(L|g|), 1),

    p_-(u,v)
      =(c-u(1+v/L), +u/(L|g|), 1).                       (1.1)

Take exactly

    c=1/20,
    u_A=9/20,
    u_B=3/20.                                              (1.2)

At `v=0` these have reference tilts

    1/2, -2/5, 1/5, -1/10,                               (1.3)

which are the four unstable parents of
`2026-09-11-source-all-orders-spectral-cage.md`.

Write

    p_1=p_+(u_A,v),   p_2=p_-(u_A,v),
    p_3=p_+(u_B,v),   p_4=p_-(u_B,v).                     (1.4)

Then for every finite `L`, every regular point with `|g|>0`, and every common
fast time for which the source phase formula is used,

    p_1+p_2=p_3+p_4=(1/10,0,2),                           (1.5)

exactly. In addition

    p_3=(2/3)p_1+(1/3)p_2,
    p_4=(1/3)p_1+(2/3)p_2.                                (1.6)

Thus the finite-`L` transverse source phase component does **not** split the two
designated daughter frequencies and does not increase the rank of the generated
frequency lattice.

This is stronger than a statement that the mismatch is small: the resonance
needed for the two-decomposition cancellation remains exact before carrier
rounding.

## 2. The complete finite-L lattice is one homogeneous shear of the cage

The frozen reference cage is

    k_(m,z)=((3m-z)/10,0,z),       m,z in Z.               (2.1)

Put

    d(k)=k_x-c k_z,
    tau=v/L,
    eta=1/(L|g|).                                         (2.2)

Define the linear map on the reference plane

    T_(tau,eta)(k)
      =(c k_z+(1+tau)d(k), -eta d(k), k_z).               (2.3)

The four parents in (1.4) are respectively

    T k_(2,1), T k_(-1,1), T k_(1,1), T k_(0,1).          (2.4)

Because `T` is linear,

    T k_(m1,z1)+T k_(m2,z2)
      =T k_(m1+m2,z1+z2).                                (2.5)

Therefore every nonlinear Fourier product of the four finite-`L` parents lies
in the **same exact rank-two integer lattice**, merely embedded by `T`.
There is no finite-`L` rank-three frequency pollution in the unrounded phase
system.

Moreover

    T k-k=(tau d(k),-eta d(k),0),                          (2.6)

so

    |T k-k|^2=d(k)^2(tau^2+eta^2).                        (2.7)

Since `c=1/20`,

    |d(k)| <= sqrt(401/400) |k|.                           (2.8)

Consequently, on every fixed fast-time window `|v|<=T_0`, and on a fixed
regular patch with `|g|>=g_0>0`,

    sup_(k!=0) |T k-k|/|k| <= C(T_0,g_0)/L.               (2.9)

For large `L` the lattice map and its inverse are therefore uniformly close to
the identity on **all** labels, not only on a finite set.

The fast-time derivative has the same uniform structure. Since

    partial_v T k=(d(k)/L,0,0),                            (2.10)

one has

    sup_(k!=0) |partial_v T k|/|T k| <= C/L.              (2.11)

This controls the pressure-normal correction generated by the moving phase.

## 3. Pressure-retaining principal operator is a uniform perturbation

The source-prehistory packet keeps the pressure constraint in the principal
amplitude equation. In normalized variables its moving-normal form is

    a'=[-K+n(n^T K-n_v^T)/|n|^2-mu |n|^2 I]a,
    n.a=0.                                                 (3.1)

At the frozen reference point, `n=k`, `n_v=0`, and the corresponding zero-order
operator is

    A_0(k)=-K_0+k(k^T K_0)/|k|^2.                         (3.2)

The finite-`L` regular-patch comparison already proved in
`2026-09-10-source-prehistory-regeneration.md` gives `K=K_0+O(L^-1)` after the
source frame normalization, while (2.9)--(2.11) control the direction and
moving-normal terms uniformly over the entire deformed lattice.

Indeed the rank-one pressure factor is the directional projector

    Pi_n=n n^T/|n|^2.                                     (3.3)

For nonzero vectors whose relative displacement is at most `epsilon<1`,

    ||Pi_n-Pi_k|| <= C epsilon.                            (3.4)

Together with (2.9)--(2.11), this gives on every fixed fast-time window

    ||A_L(v,k)-A_0(k)|| <= C(T_0,g_0,K_0)/L               (3.5)

for the zero-order part, uniformly in every nonzero lattice label.

The viscous multiplier is not treated as a bounded perturbation at high
frequency. Instead (2.9) gives

    (1-C/L)|k| <= |T k| <= (1+C/L)|k|,                    (3.6)

so the physical damping `-mu|T k|^2` remains uniformly comparable to the
reference damping. This is stronger for large labels, where viscosity dominates
the bounded stretching operator.

## 4. The all-orders spectral cage persists on a fixed local window

The frozen cage theorem has strict margins:

    gamma_u >= 1/10                                       (4.1)

on the four unstable positive branches, while every reference stable
mode/branch satisfies

    gamma_s <= -1/100.                                    (4.2)

These are strict all-lattice bounds, not a finite-depth computation.

The finite-`L` moving system retains the same four labels as its only unstable
channels for sufficiently large `L`, on every prescribed fixed fast-time window.
The proof is by a low/high split.

For sufficiently large `|k|`, the bounded zero-order source matrix is dominated
by `-mu|T k|^2`; (3.6) makes this uniformly strictly stable. This leaves only a
finite set of low reference labels. On that finite set, (3.5), continuity of
the two-dimensional transverse spectral projections, and the strict margins
(4.1)--(4.2) preserve the signs for all large `L`. Thus, after increasing
`L_0(T_0,g_0)` if needed, one may take coarse local margins such as

    unstable rate >= 1/20,
    stable rate <= -1/200.                                (4.3)

The time dependence of the corresponding two-dimensional mode splittings is
`O(L^-1)` by (2.11), so the usual finite-dimensional cone/variation-of-constants
argument gives the same finite-window exponential separation. No fifth unstable
Fourier label appears.

This is a **local nonautonomous persistence theorem**, not a claim that the
finite-`L` source coefficients remain close to the frozen reference for a
fast-time interval growing like `L` or for the whole remote past.

## 5. Full-lattice finite-horizon causal generation

The backward-eternal unstable manifold is more than is needed for one physical
entry trace. Fix a sufficiently small but positive fast duration `T_0`,
independent of `L`. Start at `v=-T_0` with a real finite Fourier sum containing
only the four unstable parent labels and their reality partners; set every
stable lattice coefficient to zero initially.

Pull the moving lattice back by `T_(tau,eta)`. Because (2.5) is exact, the full
quadratic incompressible convolution remains a convolution on the fixed integer
label lattice. On `H^r`, `r>2`, the principal coefficient system is a
nonautonomous semilinear parabolic equation with the standard estimate

    ||N_L(u)-N_L(w)||_(H^(r-1))
      <= C_r (||u||_Hr+||w||_Hr)||u-w||_Hr,               (5.1)

uniformly for large `L` on the fixed interval. Standard heat smoothing therefore
gives a unique small solution on `[-T_0,0]` retaining **every** generated stable
mode. The solution depends analytically on the four small complex parent
amplitudes and continuously (indeed smoothly in the principal coefficients) on
`L^-1`.

At `L=infinity`, let the endpoint parent products of the two designated pairs
be `w_A,w_B`. For a target branch `sigma in {+,-}`, its quadratic coefficient
at `v=0` is

    w_A beta_(A,sigma) F(D_(A,sigma),T_0)
      +w_B beta_(B,sigma) F(D_(B,sigma),T_0),              (5.2)

where

    F(D,T)=(1-exp(-D T))/D,                               (5.3)

and every `D` is the positive causal growth mismatch from the frozen-manifold
packet.

Choose

    w_A= beta_(B,+) F(D_(B,+),T_0),
    w_B=-beta_(A,+) F(D_(A,+),T_0).                       (5.4)

Then the positive target coefficient in (5.2) is exactly zero.

As `T_0 ->0+`, `F(D,T_0)/T_0 ->1`. The instantaneous four-parent theorem proves

    beta_(B,+) beta_(A,-)
      -beta_(A,+) beta_(B,-) !=0.                         (5.5)

Hence, by continuity, one may choose one fixed sufficiently small `T_0>0` for
which the negative target coefficient after (5.4) remains nonzero.

Now introduce a common small parent amplitude `epsilon`. The exact nonlinear
positive target coefficient has the analytic form

    Y_+(epsilon,r,L)
      =epsilon^2[G_+(r,T_0)+O(epsilon)+O(L^-1)],           (5.6)

where `r` is one relative pair-product parameter and the derivative of `G_+`
with respect to `r` at the quadratic cancellation is nonzero. Dividing by
`epsilon^2` and applying the ordinary implicit-function theorem gives, for all
sufficiently small `epsilon` and sufficiently large `L`, an `O(epsilon+L^-1)`
retuning of the relative phase/amplitude such that

    Y_+=0                                                  (5.7)

**for the full finite-horizon principal solution**, not merely at quadratic
order. The negative target coefficient remains

    Y_-=c_* epsilon^2+O(epsilon^3+epsilon^2/L),
    c_*!=0.                                                (5.8)

Thus one ordinary four-parent state at `v=-T_0` causally generates a pure
negative target branch at `v=0`, while every stable sideband produced in the
intervening history is retained in the same infinite-lattice solution.

No stable target or sideband is reset at an entry face.

## 6. What this closes, and the new first blocker

The following obstruction is now removed for the source principal system:

    finite-L transverse normal
      -> split daughter / rank-three lattice
      -> frozen spectral cage unusable.                   (6.1)

It does not occur. The two-decomposition resonance is exact, the full lattice
remains rank two, and the local causal full-lattice generation mechanism is
open under the actual finite-`L` principal perturbation.

The first uncontrolled step is now the **principal-to-physical lift**. One must
embed the small four-parent moving-lattice history into the complete finite-`L`
whole-space source background while retaining

* slow spatial variation of the source coefficients;
* carrier rounding and compact/Schwartz localization;
* the source mean and canonical whole-space pressure;
* radial exterior transport;
* the exact forcing-cancellation equation for the correction; and
* one physical initial trace shared with the rest of the construction.

Because the generated target is quadratic in the small parent amplitude, an
acceptable lift must track which omitted physical errors are spectrally near a
parent and which can enter the common daughter window. A generic `O(epsilon/L)`
error estimate is not by itself enough to control an `O(epsilon^2)` target; the
frequency/phase structure must be retained.

This is the next discriminating calculation. Another frozen lattice or another
finite Fourier jet would not advance the terminal route.

## 7. Scope

The theorem uses the source's unrounded principal phase laws and the
regular-patch finite-`L` principal coefficient comparison already established in
the repository. It does not assert uniformity on a fast-time interval growing
with `L`, does not identify the principal moving-lattice model with the complete
physical source, and does not prove a whole-space correction, common Schwartz
trace, singularity preservation, or `NS-R3`.
