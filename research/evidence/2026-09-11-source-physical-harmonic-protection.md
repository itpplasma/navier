## 1. Exact physical grading

On one source label, write a nonzero oscillatory component as

    a_m(x_slow,v) exp(i m Phi),        m in Z\{0}.          (1.1)

The source chooses the physical angular carrier so that the angular frequency of `exp(i Phi)` is a nonzero integer. Slow/background/mean coefficients have phase integer zero.

The source's coefficient calculus proves the following closure rules after the physical phase is evaluated:

1. slow differentiation, curls, pressure reconstruction and particular pulse propagation preserve `m`;
2. multiplication by a mean coefficient preserves `m`;
3. mean operations do not create a nonzero phase integer; and
4. products of waves add their phase integers.

These are physical-field statements, not merely auxiliary-torus bookkeeping. The source uses them to separate zero harmonics from oscillatory errors through the complete correction cycle.

The outer whole-space cutoff is axisymmetric and therefore also preserves the physical angular integer. Ordinary viscosity is rotation invariant and preserves it exactly. The canonical pressure equation has axisymmetric coefficients in the background/mean sector and likewise decouples angular Fourier integers at the linear level.

## 3. First physical errors in the daughter are quadratic

A term in harmonic `2m` must contain either

* two nonzero `m` wave factors, or
* an already existing `2m` factor multiplied by a mean.

Starting with zero `2m` correction at the left end of the local prehistory, the first mechanism is quadratic. Hence, on the fixed fast-time window of the finite-`L` theorem,

    Pi_(2m) Error_physical
       = O(epsilon^2/L)+O(epsilon^3)                      (3.1)

for the physical terms whose coefficients are `O(L^-1)` perturbations of the principal source system. The constant may depend on the fixed regular patch and the fixed fast-time window, but not on `epsilon` or sufficiently large `L`.

Axisymmetric mean corrections generated by the parents are themselves `O(epsilon^2)` and have harmonic zero. Their subsequent linear action on a parent returns harmonic `m`, not `2m`; their direct action on the daughter changes its coefficient only after the daughter exists and is therefore of higher order in the local small-data expansion. The same grading applies to pressure because the quadratic pressure source adds Fourier integers before the elliptic solve.

The finite-`L` principal theorem writes the two target coordinates after one fixed local prehistory as analytic functions of a relative pair-product parameter `r`:

    Y_+(epsilon,r,L)
      =epsilon^2[G_+(r)+O(epsilon)+O(L^-1)],              (4.1)

    Y_-(epsilon,r,L)
      =epsilon^2[G_-(r)+O(epsilon)+O(L^-1)].              (4.2)

The physical phase grading proves that adding the physical linear terms does not introduce an `epsilon/L` term into (4.1)--(4.2). They only alter the normalized functions `G_+`, `G_-` by `O(L^-1)` on the fixed window.

    G_+(r_0)=0,       partial_r G_+(r_0)!=0,
