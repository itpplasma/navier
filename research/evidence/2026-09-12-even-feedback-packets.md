# Small odd data do not bound the generated even-strain action

Date: 2026-09-12. Input: `itpplasma/navier@c7feb2cabf2329fcd5cff69165cb110d7c289903`.

**Author analytic proof; independent audit and novelty assessment pending.**
The solutions in this control are original, unforced, fixed-viscosity,
whole-space Navier--Stokes solutions, globally smooth from compactly supported
solenoidal data. The even component is zero initially and is generated by the
odd component. Neither a prescribed forcing nor an independently supplied
even field is used.

The conclusion is an obstruction to a norm-only estimate, not to global
regularity: even arbitrarily small L3 data can have arbitrarily large
accumulated supremum strain in the generated even component. Higher Schwartz
seminorms are not uniformly bounded. No single singular solution is produced.

## Working packet

TERMINAL CLAIM: the unchanged original unforced NS problem on R3, nu>0,
Schwartz solenoidal initial data and its classical branch.

ESTABLISHED: the weighted physical pump estimate leaves the even-feedback
budget B_Z, which includes integral ||S(Z)||_infinity. The all-mode pressure
adapter additionally leaves J_gap; no exact symmetry reset is permissible.

FIRST GAP: can small odd energy, dissipation or a small critical L3 norm alone
provide the missing even-feedback action bound?

PREDICTION BEFORE TEST: no. Each small odd packet generates an even response
of quadratic size. Distinct parabolic scales can place these responses in
disjoint time windows. Cubed L3 norms add across separated packets, whereas
the accumulated generated action adds quadratically. These summabilities differ.

FALSIFIER: vanishing of the full projected quadratic even response; cancellation
when all spatial tails and interactions are retained; or a uniform small-L3
estimate preventing the proposed quadratic action accumulation.

CHECK: explicit compact odd seed, complete Leray quadratic response,
small-data whole-space mild theory, finite-profile decoupling and smooth
spacetime test functions. No numerical PDE approximation is used.

## 1. Precise obstruction

Fix nu>0 and H>0. Let R=diag(-1,-1,1) be rotation through pi about the x3 axis,
and define the rotational action and even projection by

    (Rcal u)(x)=R u(R^(-1)x),     E(u)=(u+Rcal u)/2.

There exists a sequence d_N in C_c^infinity(R3;R3), div d_N=0, with

    supp d_N contained in the unit ball,
    Rcal d_N=-d_N,
    ||d_N||_3 -> 0,       ||d_N||_2 -> 0,                (1)

whose original unforced solutions u_N are globally smooth and satisfy

    int_0^H ||S(E(u_N)(t))||_infinity dt -> infinity.     (2)

The sequence can moreover be chosen with u_N(t,0)=0 and with the integrand
in (2) replaced by |S(E(u_N))(t,0)|; Section 7 supplies this material version.

Here S(v)=(grad v+grad v^T)/2; matrix norms can be any fixed equivalent norm.
In particular E(u_N)(0)=0 exactly. By the energy identity,

    sup_t ||u_N(t)||_2^2
       +2nu int_0^infinity ||grad u_N||_2^2
          <= ||d_N||_2^2 ->0.                           (3)

The same upper bounds, separately, apply to the even and odd projections.
The small-data solutions also have uniformly small critical spacetime L5 norm.

An equivalent convenient version is: for every sufficiently small delta>0,
energy tolerance e>0 and action target M>0, a compact odd datum can satisfy
||d||_3<delta, ||d||_2<e and (2)>M. Thus no locally bounded function of upper
bounds on initial L3, initial L2, energy dissipation, nu and H controls (2).
This does not exclude bounds depending on higher initial norms or on the
specific source history.

## 2. Analytic tools and their scope

Only the standard small-data whole-space mild theory is imported. For fixed
nu>0, sufficiently small divergence-free L3 data have a unique global mild
solution, preserving higher smooth regularity of the initial datum. On smooth
compact data it is the classical branch with canonical pressure and the usual
energy identity. The source-inspected interface is recorded below.

For clarity, the estimates used in the packet decoupling can be obtained
without a profile-decomposition theorem. Put

    X=L5((0,infinity) x R3),
    B(u,v)(t)=-int_0^t e^(nu(t-s)Delta) P div(u tensor v)(s) ds.

There is a constant C_nu such that

    ||e^(nu t Delta)d||_X <= C_nu ||d||_3,
    ||B(u,v)||_X <= C_nu ||u||_X ||v||_X.                (4)

Here and below the same letter C_nu can be increased by fixed factors.
The heat estimate follows by applying the scalar heat energy estimate to
|v|^(3/2), interpolating L5 between L3 and L9, and integrating:

    int ||v||_5^5 <= C_nu ||d||_3^5.

For the second estimate, the kernel of e^(nu t Delta)P div is bounded by
C_nu (|x|+sqrt(t))^(-4) for t>0. In spacetime with parabolic dimension five,
this is a potential of order one. The strong fractional-integration estimate
maps L^(5/2) into L5, since 1/5=2/5-1/5. Holder then gives (4).
All exponents are strictly inside the fractional-integration range; no
endpoint strong convolution inequality is invoked.

The small-data contraction gives ||u||_X<=2C_nu||d||_3. More generally, if a
small approximate mild trajectory V has exact heat datum d and defect e,

    V=e^(nu t Delta)d+B(V,V)+e,

then the exact small mild solution obeys

    ||u-V||_X <= 2||e||_X,                              (5)

provided C_nu(||u||_X+||V||_X)<=1/2. This follows immediately by subtracting
the two equations and expanding B(u,u)-B(V,V). No derivative convergence is
asserted by (5); the strain lower bound below uses distributional testing.

Analytic dependence on a scalar initial amplitude, on a fixed short classical
interval, follows from the same quadratic heat contraction in C_t H^s,
with one-derivative parabolic smoothing, for any sufficiently large fixed s.
The resulting expansion and its spatial derivatives are uniform for amplitudes
in one sufficiently small fixed neighborhood of zero.

## 3. A compact odd seed with a nonzero full quadratic even response

Choose a smooth radial cutoff chi(|x|^2) equal to one on a nonempty ball and
compactly supported. Set

    psi=x1^2 x2 chi(|x|^2),
    f=(partial_2 psi,-partial_1 psi,0).                 (6)

Then f is smooth, compact, solenoidal and Rcal f=-f. On the region chi=1,

    f=(x1^2,-2x1 x2,0),
    (f.grad)f=(2x1^3,2x1^2 x2,0),
    [curl((f.grad)f)]_3=4x1 x2.                         (7)

Consequently g=-P[(f.grad)f] is nonzero: Leray projection removes a gradient,
which has zero curl. Also S(g) is nonzero. Indeed a solenoidal g in H1 obeys
2||S(g)||_2^2=||grad g||_2^2, so zero strain would force the L2 field g to vanish.

Let u^a be the solution with datum af, for small real a. On a fixed short
classical interval,

    u^a=a v+a^2 z_2+O(a^3),
    v=e^(nu t Delta)f,
    z_2=B(v,v),        partial_t z_2(0)=g.              (8)

This is the full quadratic Duhamel coefficient, with canonical Leray pressure.
Rotational covariance and uniqueness give Rcal u^a=u^(-a). Hence the exact
even field has the sharper parity expansion

    E^a=(u^a+u^(-a))/2=a^2 z_2+O(a^4).                 (9)

All subsequent sidebands are retained in u^a, not dropped from the equation.

Choose a smooth compact symmetric matrix test H0 with ||H0||_L1=1 and
<H0,S(g)> >0. Equation (8) permits a fixed tau>0 and a nonnegative
beta in C_c^infinity((tau,2tau)), beta<=1, for which the number

    c_* = int beta(t)<H0,S(z_2(t))>dt

is strictly positive. Uniform analytic dependence in (9), after decreasing
a_*>0 if needed, implies for 0<a<=a_*

    int beta(t)<H0,S(E^a(t))>dt >= (c_*/2) a^2.         (10)

The left side is at most int_tau^(2tau)||S(E^a(t))||_infinity dt.
The constants tau,c_*,a_* depend only on f,nu and the chosen tests, not on
any subsequent packet count, translation or scaling.

## 4. Finite separated packets are approximated by their full nonlinear flows

Fix a finite positive integer N, amplitudes a<=a_*, and scales

    lambda_j=Lambda 2^j,        1<=j<=N.

For separation parameter D>0 choose y_j=jD e3 and let

    d_(N,D)(x)=sum_j a lambda_j f(lambda_j(x-y_j)).       (11)

For sufficiently large D the initial supports are disjoint. Translations
along the rotation axis and isotropic scalings commute with Rcal, so (11)
is exactly odd. The initial norm identities are

    ||d_(N,D)||_3^3=N a^3 ||f||_3^3,
    ||d_(N,D)||_2^2=a^2 ||f||_2^2 sum_j lambda_j^(-1).   (12)

Let U_j be each complete isolated nonlinear solution,

    U_j(t,x)=lambda_j u^a(lambda_j^2 t,lambda_j(x-y_j)),
    V_D=sum_j U_j.

For fixed N,a,lambda_j and D->infinity, spatial translation and Holder give

    ||U_i tensor U_j||_(L^(5/2)_(t,x)) ->0  (i!=j),
    ||V_D||_X^5 ->sum_j ||U_j||_X^5=N||u^a||_X^5.       (13)

For example, approximate the finitely many unshifted L5 profiles by compact
spacetime functions. Their translations eventually have disjoint spatial
supports; Holder bounds the approximation errors. This proves both limits,
including the noncompact pressure tails of the actual profiles.

The defect in the approximate mild equation for V_D is exactly the sum of
cross terms B(U_i,U_j), i!=j. By (4) and (13), its X norm tends to zero.
There is no external forcing in the exact solution u_(N,D) with datum (11).
For sufficiently small total initial L3 norm and small ||V_D||_X, (5) proves

    ||u_(N,D)-V_D||_X ->0.                              (14)

The limits in (13)--(14) are taken for each *finite* N. No uniform-separation
limit at infinite N, or infinite sum of initial data, is used.

## 5. The strain-action lower bound needs only the convergence in (14)

The scaled response windows

    I_j=[tau/lambda_j^2, 2tau/lambda_j^2]

are disjoint, since the next upper endpoint is half the current lower endpoint.
Choose Lambda large enough that all I_j lie in (0,H). Put

    beta_j(t)=beta(lambda_j^2 t),
    H_j(x)=lambda_j^3 H0(lambda_j(x-y_j)).

The spatial L1 norm of H_j is one. For any smooth field w,

    |int beta_j <H_j,S(w)> dt|
          <=int_(I_j) ||S(w)||_infinity dt.              (15)

On the isolated field U_j the left pairing, with w=E(U_j), is exactly the
left side of (10). Strain has scaling factor lambda_j^2 and time measure has
factor lambda_j^(-2), so those factors cancel.

For the error in (14) integrate the strain onto the compact test:

    int beta_j <H_j,S(E(error))>dt
       =-int beta_j div H_j . E(error) dxdt.            (16)

The L^(5/4) norm of beta_j div H_j is scale invariant:
it has amplitude lambda_j^4 and spacetime volume factor lambda_j^(-5).
Holder therefore bounds (16) by a fixed test constant times ||error||_X.
The even projection is an L5 contraction. Thus (14) transfers each pairing
without incorrectly differentiating an L5 error.

The other isolated profiles have vanishing pairings near y_j as D->infinity,
again by compact testing and spatial translation of L5 fields. For this finite
collection choose D so large that the sum of all errors in every pairing is
less than c_* a^2/4. Equations (10), (15)--(16) then give

    int_(I_j) ||S(E(u_(N,D)))||_infinity dt >= c_* a^2/4,

    int_0^H ||S(E(u_(N,D)))||_infinity dt >= c_* N a^2/4. (17)

Every term on the left is measured in one exact unforced solution initialized
once, at time zero. There are no pulse resets and no discarded cross terms.

## 6. Choose the amplitudes and obtain (1)--(3)

For fixed small a0, set a=a0 N^(-1/3). The cubed L3 norm in (12) is a0^3
||f||_3^3, while (17) grows as c a0^2 N^(1/3). Also

    sum_j lambda_j^(-1) < Lambda^(-1).

The approximate trajectory in (13) stays small: its limiting fifth power is
O(Na^5)=O(a0^5 N^(-2/3)). Thus the smallness used in (14) holds for every N
with a0 chosen sufficiently small once.

A stronger choice is

    a=a0 N^(-5/12).

It gives

    ||d_(N,D)||_3=a0 ||f||_3 N^(-1/12) ->0,
    int_0^H ||S(E(u_(N,D)))||_infinity dt
                            >=c a0^2 N^(1/6) ->infinity, (18)
    ||d_(N,D)||_2^2 <= C a0^2 N^(-5/6)/Lambda ->0.

After choosing the finite D for each N, one final *common* NS dilation

    d_N(x)=sigma_N d_(N,D)(sigma_N x),
    u_N(t,x)=sigma_N u_(N,D)(sigma_N^2 t,sigma_N x)

places the entire compact support in the unit ball. Choose sigma_N>=1 large
enough for this. L3 is unchanged, L2 decreases by sigma_N^(-1/2), and the
response windows move earlier, remaining inside (0,H). Their integrated
supremum strain is unchanged. This proves (1)--(2); the energy identity proves
(3). The common dilation also permits arbitrarily small initial energy at any
fixed action target.

Each d_N is individually smooth and compactly supported. The sequence is not
bounded in all Schwartz seminorms and has no asserted smooth limiting datum
with infinite action. The response windows accumulate toward the initial time
as N increases; this is not a positive-time blowup construction.

## 7. The obstruction also occurs along one stationary fluid particle

The preceding proof uses separated spatial packets. The failure is not cured
by replacing the moving spatial supremum by the action along one material
trajectory: the construction can instead use nested scales at one center.
Here are the additional details, including a nonzero pointwise coefficient.

Start temporarily with the Schwartz streamfunction

    psi=x1^2 x2 x3 exp(-|x|^2),     f=curl(psi e3).

Its explicit components, after stripping exp(-|x|^2), are

    f1=x1^2 x3(1-2x2^2),
    f2=2x1 x2 x3(x1^2-1),     f3=0.                    (19)

This field has both Rcal f=-f and f(-x)=-f(x). The latter symmetry is preserved
by original NS (covariance under spatial inversion and uniqueness), so every
solution built from centered dilations of this seed has u(t,0)=0. The origin
is one stationary Lagrangian trajectory, not a sequence of different particles.

Let p solve -Delta p=partial_i partial_j(f_i f_j). Because f vanishes to
third order at the origin, the full quadratic acceleration g=-P div(f tensor f)
has

    S(g)(0)=-Hess p(0)
           =diag(1/195,29/3003,-74/5005).               (20)

This includes the nonlocal pressure exactly. For verification, integrate the
fourth derivative of Gamma(x)=1/(4pi|x|) against f_i f_j. Its kernel is

    4pi partial_abij Gamma
      =105 x_a x_b x_i x_j/r^9
       -15( delta_ab x_i x_j + delta_ai x_b x_j
           +delta_aj x_b x_i +delta_bi x_a x_j
           +delta_bj x_a x_i +delta_ij x_a x_b )/r^7
       +3( delta_ab delta_ij+delta_ai delta_bj
                              +delta_aj delta_bi )/r^5. (21)

The stress vanishes to sixth order, so the integral is absolutely convergent
at zero and the local distribution terms do not contribute. Odd monomials
integrate to zero. For nonnegative even integers A,B,C, with d=A+B+C and
q=(d-k+3)/2>0, the remaining exact rule is

    int x1^A x2^B x3^C r^(-k) exp(-2r^2) dx /(4pi)
      =[2 prod_j Gamma((A_j+1)/2)/Gamma((d+3)/2)]
                              *Gamma(q)/(2*2^q)/(4pi). (22)

Equations (19), (21)--(22) give (20) with rational coefficients. The trace
is zero, providing another check. In particular the 11 component is positive.

One may make the seed compact before continuing: multiply psi by a smooth
radial cutoff equal to one on a ball of radius R0 and zero outside twice
that ball. As R0 tends to infinity these streamfunctions, their curls and
the full projected quadratic accelerations converge in all needed Sobolev
norms. Hence S(g)(0) converges to (20). Fix one finite sufficiently large R0
so its 11 component is still positive. Both symmetries are retained exactly.
Use that one fixed compact seed for everything that follows.

Analytic dependence and (9) now give a fixed short interval I=(tau,2tau) and
constant c>0 with

    int_I S(E^a)(t,0)_(11) dt >= c a^2.                 (23)

Choose centered data with lambda_j=Lambda R^j, R>=2, and no translations:

    d_(N,R)=sum_(j=1)^N a lambda_j f(lambda_j x).

For every fixed N, as R->infinity, critical-scale orthogonality gives

    ||d_(N,R)||_3^3 -> N a^3 ||f||_3^3,
    ||sum_j U_j||_X^5 ->N||u^a||_X^5,
    ||U_i tensor U_j||_(5/2) ->0  (i!=j).               (24)

These statements follow by the same density argument as (13): approximate L3
functions by functions on compact spatial annuli, and X functions by functions
on compact *parabolic* annuli bounded away from (t,x)=(0,0). Widely different
dilations separate those supports. The norm measure factors are exactly zero
in L3 and X. This proves (24) directly, without importing a general nonlinear
profile-decomposition theorem. Equation (5) again gives a global X error tending
to zero for the exact solution, for each finite N.

To transfer a pointwise strain rather than a compact test, use the following
standard positive-time smoothing consequence of the small-data contraction:

    sup_(tau/2<=t<=3tau) ||grad^k u(t)||_infinity
                         <= C_(nu,tau,k) ||d||_3,       (25)

for k up to any fixed finite integer. The constants are independent of the
Schwartz seminorms of d. One proof first contracts in the Kato space
sup t^(1/4)||u(t)||_6, where the quadratic kernel is (t-s)^(-3/4)s^(-1/2).
On intervals away from zero, the mild equation then improves L6 to L12 and
L-infinity by integrable heat kernels. Spatial Holder regularity followed by
differentiated heat estimates gives (25). Only the globally small-data class
is used; (25) is not claimed at arbitrary critical amplitude.

Normalize around any j-th scale. The X error is unchanged by this rescaling.
The normalized exact solution satisfies (25) because its initial L3 norm is
unchanged. The finite sum of normalized isolated profiles has the same bounds
with a constant allowed to depend on N. Every *other* isolated profile has
L5 norm tending to zero on compact spacetime cylinders with positive lower
time: in its own coordinates the time interval tends either to zero or to
infinity, and its global L5 integral is finite.

These local L5 convergences and uniform spatial second-derivative bounds imply
convergence of the time-integrated gradient at the origin. Explicitly, on a
fixed spatial ball, mollification with radius h gives

    |grad e(t,0)| <= C h^(-8/5)||e(t)||_(L5(ball))
                                      +C h||grad^2 e(t)||_infinity.

Optimization and Holder in time show that the integrated gradient tends to
zero with the local spacetime L5 norm; the second-derivative bound is uniform.
This argument avoids differentiating the X convergence without a smoothing
estimate. It also avoids claiming uniform control as N tends to infinity:
R is chosen after each finite N.

Consequently, for sufficiently large finite R=R_N, (23) transfers to every
rescaled disjoint window I_j, giving

    int_0^H |S(E(u_(N,R_N)))(t,0)| dt >= c' N a^2.       (26)

Take again a=a0 N^(-5/12). Then the initial L3 norm tends to zero and (26)
grows at least as N^(1/6). The L2 norm tends to zero by the triangle bound

    ||d_(N,R_N)||_2 <= a||f||_2 sum_j lambda_j^(-1/2)
                                 <= C a||f||_2 Lambda^(-1/2).

Choose Lambda once large enough to put all initial compact supports in the
unit ball and all windows inside (0,H). Both the half-turn odd symmetry and
full inversion odd symmetry hold for every datum and its actual solution.
Thus the material path in (26) is exactly x(t)=0. In particular the same
norm-only obstruction remains for accumulated even strain along a fixed
fluid particle, not just for the global supremum.

## 8. Consequence for the live even-feedback dependency

Take zero prescribed base/pump and phi=0 for this control. The nonlinear even
feedback is Z=E(u_N), initially zero, and the previously defined B_Z includes
exactly int||S(E(u_N))||_infinity. Thus B_Z cannot be bounded uniformly from
small odd initial energy/dissipation or small initial L3 alone. The obstruction
already occurs in a globally regular small-data class.

What is blacklisted is the inference

    small critical/energy norm of the odd sector
       + initially zero even sector
       -> uniformly small accumulated even Lipschitz action.

This does NOT blacklist the full-state approach, frequency-sensitive estimates,
source-specific geometry, signed energy exchange, localized material budgets,
or bounds involving stronger initial data. Nor does it establish that the
actual source's B_Z is large. It prevents substituting the generic available
norms for a proof of that source-specific budget.

After the approximate-gap adapter, J_gap and B_Z still need one-history
producers. Two scalar/norm-only repairs now end at a precise wall: low-mode
pressure costs an exponential weight factor, and critical/energy smallness
alone does not control the generated even strain action. The next material
change is to retain signed *coupled* even/odd exchange in a full-state energy,
rather than bounding even feedback by its standalone supremum strain.
