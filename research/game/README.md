# Executable math-frontier game

Thin executable backend for the repository's existing research discipline. It does not replace `PLAN.md` or `docs/proof-graph.yaml`.

Commands:

    python3 research/game/run.py status
    python3 research/game/run.py fast
    python3 research/game/run.py replay --model baseline
    python3 research/game/run.py discriminate baseline refuted_peak_strain
    python3 research/game/run.py check-case clock-action-obstruction
    python3 research/game/run.py full

`fast` fails closed when a pinned authoritative source changes, then replays all frozen controls.

For Navier, exact analytic identities, exact symbolic/scaling contradictions, audited theorem interfaces and rigorous estimates may falsify mechanisms at their stated scope. Floating numerical PDE experiments are heuristic or discriminating evidence only and cannot by themselves prove regularity or blowup.

Current routes are priorities, not a whitelist. New mechanisms are encouraged. After two serious returns to the same uncontrolled critical quantity or exact obstruction, change mechanism, abstraction, representation or route.
